# Varifold

**Infrastructure Research for Verifiable Knowledge Discovery**

Varifold is a research lab exploring the mathematical and computational foundations of verifiable knowledge discovery and AI safety.

Our research spans formal verification, interpretability, automated crowdsourcing markets, and incentive design, alongside the formal modeling and verification of economic and financial systems. We study how humans and AI agents produce, verify, and exchange knowledge, and how the mechanisms coordinating them can be understood and made reliable.

We use mathematical analysis, Lean-based formalization, and computational experiments. Application areas include DeSci tokenomics, computational resource accounting, and settlement mechanisms.
