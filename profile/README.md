# Varifold

Varifold is an open learning and research community exploring the mathematical foundations of intelligent systems. We study formal verification, AI safety, and multi-agent coordination, and develop open-source notes, models, and tools with explicit assumptions and checkable claims.

## Research directions

- Mathematical foundations and formal verification.
- AI safety and neural network specifications.
- Multi-agent coordination and economic systems.

## Working together

Contribute a clear explanation, a worked example, a reproducible experiment, or a machine-checked proof. We distinguish proposed ideas, implemented behavior, and verified properties.

[ν-DSI](https://github.com/Varifold-Lab/vdsi) explores verified decentralized coordination through a Lean prototype. Our other projects develop mathematical libraries, model specifications, algorithms, and learning resources.
