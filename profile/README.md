# Varifold

**Infrastructure Research for Verifiable Knowledge Discovery**

Varifold is a research lab studying automated crowdsourcing markets for formal verification, incentive mechanisms, and the formal modeling of economic and financial systems.

We develop open-source models, machine-checked proofs, and executable experiments in Lean to study how knowledge is produced, verified, and rewarded. Our research includes DeSci tokenomics and verifiable resource accounting and settlement.
